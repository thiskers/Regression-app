About this app

Regression-app is a single-file browser tool for statistical regression analysis. Load a sample or your own CSV, fit OLS / polynomial / logistic models, inspect a full diagnostic suite, and read a plain-English interpretation of what the model says.

Numerical accuracy is validated against the NIST StRD reference datasets. Open Run self-tests (footer) once Step 15 has landed.
